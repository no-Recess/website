Working with playlists

Use the Playlist panel to add music and organize it into groups. A playlist is a separate collection selected from the menu at the top. Groups are folders inside that playlist, and can contain tracks and other groups.

Add music

  • Drag audio files, CUE sheets or music folders from Finder into noRecess.
  • Alternatively, click the Add tracks button (file with a plus) and choose Files… or Folder….
  • Choose the destination playlist before importing. Folder and CUE imports can create groups in the playlist.

A CUE sheet describes tracks within audio files; keep it together with the audio it references. If macOS prompts for folder access, select the folder containing those files. For older CUE sheets, a CUE Text Encoding dialog may appear: choose the preview with readable names and finish the import. Finish or cancel that dialog before adding more files.

Create, switch and rename playlists

  1. Open the playlist-name menu at the top of the panel.
  2. Choose New Playlist…, enter a name and confirm to create a separate collection.
  3. Choose an existing name from the same menu to switch collections.
  4. To change the current playlist's name, choose Rename Playlist….

Renaming a playlist changes its name in noRecess; it does not rename your music files.

Create and rename groups

  1. Click Create group (folder with a plus).
  2. Enter a name and click Create. Move tracks into the new group by dragging them.
  3. To rename a group, select only that group, then right-click and choose Rename, or press F2. Some Mac keyboards need Fn + F2.

Use the small plus/minus box next to a group to expand or collapse it. The Collapse or expand groups toolbar menu offers Collapse all and Expand all.

Select and play tracks

  • Click a row to select it.
  • Command-click to add or remove individual rows from the selection.
  • Shift-click to select a range of visible rows.
  • Double-click a track to start playback. A single click selects it without starting playback.

The current track is highlighted in green. Find current track (magnifying glass) expands its groups, selects it and scrolls it into view.

Move tracks and groups with drag and drop

What you want to doWhere to drop
Reorder tracksDrag a track or several selected tracks above or below another track.
Move tracks into a groupDrop selected tracks onto the middle of the group's row.
Reorder groupsDrag one group near the top or bottom edge of another group's row.
Nest a group inside anotherDrag one group onto the middle of another group's row.
Move items out to the top levelDrop tracks or one group into the empty space below the tree.

Watch the insertion line or target highlight before releasing the mouse. Moving a group moves its contents with it. Move tracks and groups separately: mixed selections and several groups at once cannot be dragged. A group cannot be moved inside itself or one of its descendants.

These operations rearrange the current playlist. They do not move files or folders on your disk.

Sorting, filtering, favorites and context menus

  • Sort playlist [A-Z] sorts groups and tracks alphabetically after confirmation, replacing the current manual order.
  • Right-click a group and choose Sort inside [A-Z] to sort that group.
  • Enable Show playlist filter in the playlist settings to display the filter field. Type text to narrow the visible tree; clear it to show the full list again.
  • Use the heart beside a track to mark or unmark it as a favorite. Hover over the row to reveal its control.
  • Right-click a track or group to Copy Name, reveal an associated audio file in Finder or Remove it. For a group, Finder reveals an associated track's file.

Cassette side separators, when enabled, show the tape layout. Organize your library using the group rows and track rows.

Remove tracks, groups or all playlist contents

  • Select the rows, then click the trash button, press Delete or right-click and choose Remove. Review the confirmation before proceeding.
  • Removing a group also removes its contents from the playlist.
  • To empty the current playlist, open its name menu and choose Clear Playlist…, then confirm.

Your original audio files are not deleted. Removing or clearing changes the list in noRecess.
