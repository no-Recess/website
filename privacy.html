Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes the Mac App Store version of noRecess.

Your music and local data

noRecess reads audio files and artwork that you select or allow it to access. Playlists, file paths, track metadata, favorites, playback state, settings and file-access permissions are stored locally on your Mac. The developer does not receive your music library through the app.

Analytics and advertising

The Mac App Store version does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics, tracking or automatic log uploads. Local diagnostic logs may contain file paths and technical playback information.

macOS media integration

noRecess provides the current track's title, artist, album, artwork and playback state to macOS for Now Playing and system media controls.

Optional purchases

Optional tips are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives product and transaction information to verify and complete purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details through noRecess. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you contact support, your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send are used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send sensitive information or music files unless necessary. You may contact us about deletion of information you have sent.

Local data and choices

You can remove playlist entries and saved folder access in the app. Removing a playlist entry does not delete your original audio file. Local settings and app data are stored in the macOS application container; removing the app alone may leave that data on your Mac.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and do not include custom analytics, tracking scripts or forms. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Privacy questions: norecess.support@gmail.com

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change.
