Support

noRecess is a local music player for macOS with a cassette-inspired interface.

Contact

Contact: norecess.support@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include your macOS version, noRecess version, the audio format and the steps needed to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful. Remove private file paths and personal information before sharing logs. You do not need to send your music files.

Getting started

Drag audio files or a music folder into the Playlist panel. For CUE albums, allow access to the folder containing the referenced audio files when prompted.

Keyboard controls

Use these while noRecess has keyboard focus and you are not entering text.

KeyAction
SpacePlay / pause
B / ZNext / previous track
. / ,Seek forward / backward 5 seconds
= or + / −Increase / decrease volume

Headphone controls

Compatible headphones, including AirPods and EarPods, can control playback and track switching through macOS media controls. Gestures depend on your headphone model and settings.

Optional tips

The Support button in the app offers optional tips through Apple. Purchases do not unlock features. For payment or refund questions, use Apple's purchase support.
